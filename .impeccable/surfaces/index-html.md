# Map (index.html)

Scope: the single app surface. A map and a list of every PH credit card promo. Mode: **Operate**.
Audience/job: cardholders deciding where to eat or shop right now, on a phone. Their task is to filter to their banks and find nearby deals and how long each one lasts.
Constraints: the list mirrors the map for keyboard and screen-reader users; online/nationwide promos get their own tab; data freshness is shown per bank; approximate pins are marked.

Ranked grounded list (Filipino world): 1 wallet card stack · 2 mall "You are here" directory kiosk · 3 POS charge slip · 4 department-store red sale tag · 5 jeepney route signboard · 6 perforated coupon booklet · 7 sari-sari hand-lettered cardboard price sign.
Unattended run: the owner asked for no questions, so no decision page was served. Challenger verdicts: airport yellow wayfinding (competitive, holds clarity); Japanese high-density (declined); boarding pass/gate board (declined); lexicon plate (declined); timetable rack (declined); split-flap (declined).

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every promo is a hand-lettered price card from a sari-sari store, with the discount in marker as the biggest thing on screen, taped onto a paper map. Refuses the category default of bank-banner grids and blue fintech chrome.
OWN-WORLD: Kraft cardboard ground, manila-paper cards, marker-black ink, sale red reserved for discounts and "ending soon", pale masking-tape strips as the chip and label carrier. A bank's colour appears only inside its small rubber-stamp mark (raise from the lexicon: colour quarantined in plates). The list is packed tight on ruled rows (raise from the Japanese high-density site). Days left is a tabular-figure column, so ranking reads like a board (raise from the gate board).
STORY: I tick my banks once, see marker numbers pinned near me, tap one, read the merchant, the eligible cards and the days left, and open the bank's page.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed recoloured paper map. A taped wallet strip of bank chips sits along the top edge. Pins become small cardboard price tags ("20%") once zoomed in; clusters are round count stickers. On phones, a bottom sheet titled "N deals here" lists price cards; on desktop it's a 400px left rail. The primary action is the wallet strip.
FORM: sari-sari cardboard price sign, position 7 of 7, seed 675d6531.
SIGNATURE: tapping a pin tapes its price card up. The card drops in at a slight tilt, settles straight, and a tape strip lands across its top. Reduced motion gets an instant swap.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
